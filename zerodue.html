🔔 Enable notifications to get daily bill reminders.

Add card

If the due day falls in the following month (e.g. generated on the 25th, due on the 12th), it's handled automatically. Reminders run daily from the generation date until you mark the bill paid — including overdue.

Settings

How reminders work: once a day, after your chosen time, the app notifies you about every unpaid bill. The check runs while the app is open and every time you open it — so install it to your home screen and open it once a day. For reminders when the app is fully closed, see the sw.js note at the bottom of this file's source.
